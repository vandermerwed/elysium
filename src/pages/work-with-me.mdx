I'm Daniel van der Merwe, a senior technologist with 18 years on the .NET and Optimizely stack. I'm the founder of [Rokkit200](https://rokkit200.co), a software agency in South Africa. My focus is how engineering teams and individuals actually adopt AI, the operating model, the practice, and the tooling, not the slide deck.

## Talks

Practical sessions for engineering teams and leadership groups. My current talk is "Software for one," on building personal software with AI agents. I also speak on [the five levels of organisational AI maturity](https://rokkit200.co/insights/five-levels-of-organisational-ai-maturity).

## Workshops

Half-day and full-day working sessions for teams that want to move from experimenting with AI coding tools to an actual engineering practice. We cover conventions, review, agents, and guardrails.

## Advisory

Short engagements for technology leaders working out where AI fits in their delivery organisation, or who need a second pair of eyes on an architecture or platform decision.
